O mnie
I engineer complete AI systems from zero to production. Solo. No team, no funding, no excuses — just architecture decisions, shipped code, and systems that work in the real world.
​My core obsession is stateful AI, intent detection, and bridging the gap between "standard" tools and what users actually need:

​🧠 Persistent Memory (ANIMA): Most AI is stateless and forgetful. I build memory engines that give agents a long-term "soul," isolating context with production-grade precision. I don’t believe in hallucinations; I believe in hardcoded grounding.

​🔴 Intent Intelligence (LDI): I focus on the "thinking pause." My systems capture user intent before the Enter key is even touched. It’s about turning invisible, lost behavior into actionable data.

​🌍 Data Advocacy (Skankran): I take messy, unstructured PDF data and turn it into health-first intelligence. I believe data should serve people, not pipe longevity, which is why I enforce WHO/EPA standards over local legal minimums.

​⚡ The Hacker Mindset (XHR/DOM Injection): I don't wait for official API features. When I needed to make 3rd-party AI interfaces stateful, I reverse-engineered their communication. I built a layer that intercepts XHR streams to inject my own RAG context. If a tool limits my vision, I engineer a way around those limits.

​📡 Anti-Legacy Engineering: I built my own event-tracking systems because I was sick of GA4’s latency and over-complicated interfaces. I build for speed and clarity, not for corporate compliance. Again - if a tool limits my vision, i look for a better replacement.
